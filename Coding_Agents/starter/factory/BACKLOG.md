# Backlog

Ideas from club members. Pick one and give it to `$factory-intake` in your own words.

## First: build the game

0. **Build Snake** (the workshop ticket): "Build the Snake game. The rules are in README.md. Fill in the TODOs in snake/game.py and snake/app.py."

## Make Snake better

1. **Wrap-around walls:** "Add a wrap-around mode. When the snake goes off one edge of the board, it comes back on the opposite edge instead of dying. Turn it on with `--wrap`."
2. **High score:** "Save the best score to a file and show it next to the current score. It should still be there after I close the game."
3. **Speed up:** "Every 5 food, make the snake a little faster, up to a top speed. Show the level."
4. **Pause:** "Press P or Space to pause and resume. Show PAUSED while it's paused."
5. **Golden apple:** "Sometimes a golden apple appears for 5 seconds. It's worth 5 points."
6. **Obstacles:** "Add a few walls inside the board. Each new game puts them in different places, and never on the snake or the food."

## Other snake games to try

7. **Two players:** "Two snakes on one board, one on the arrow keys and one on WASD. If your head hits the other snake, you lose."
8. **Light cycles (Tron):** "The snake never shrinks: its tail stays where it's been. Survive the longest."
9. **Nibbles levels:** "Like the old QBasic Nibbles: when you eat 10 food, move on to a new level with a different maze."
10. **Hungry snake:** "The snake shrinks by one square every 3 seconds if it hasn't eaten. At length 1, game over."
